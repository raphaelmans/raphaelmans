## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The homepage is a concise qualified-interview brief
The homepage SHALL establish Raphael's identity, current role, senior product-engineering positioning, strongest credibility bridge, and primary evaluation action without requiring the visitor to read full case-study detail.

#### Scenario: Thirty-second hiring scan
- **WHEN** a hiring manager scans the opening and first proof sections on desktop or at 390px
- **THEN** they can identify who Raphael is, the level and kind of work sought, the current role, one differentiating evidence signal, and the next action for evaluating fit

#### Scenario: Server-rendered orientation
- **WHEN** the homepage is inspected before client-side interaction
- **THEN** the complete identity, role-fit statement, credibility bridge, and primary action are present in semantic document order

### Requirement: Featured projects use a comparable four-part argument
Each homepage project SHALL present no more than four primary evidence layers: direct context or bounded outcome, one attributable consequential decision, one meaningful proof artifact, and one primary case-study action.

#### Scenario: Project comparison without technology tags
- **WHEN** a visitor compares all featured projects without reading technology metadata
- **THEN** each project still communicates what mattered, what Raphael decided or owned, what artifact proves it, and where to inspect the full study

#### Scenario: Employer contribution remains bounded
- **WHEN** an employer-delivered project is summarized in the compact format
- **THEN** its context or decision copy preserves employer attribution and does not imply sole product ownership

### Requirement: Long-form evidence remains in case studies
Dedicated boundary notes, complete technology inventories, detailed artifact captions, reliability depth, and extended implementation narrative SHALL live in published case studies rather than being repeated in full on the homepage.

#### Scenario: Homepage progressive disclosure
- **WHEN** a project has detailed limitations, multiple decisions, or more than a concise technology summary
- **THEN** the homepage provides a clear case-study transition instead of expanding those details inline

#### Scenario: Case-study continuity
- **WHEN** a visitor follows a homepage project action
- **THEN** the case study preserves the fuller reviewed evidence needed to evaluate attribution, constraints, decisions, reliability, and limitations

### Requirement: The strongest ready project leads Selected Work
Selected Work SHALL lead with the project that is complete, reachable, current enough to discuss, and strongest at demonstrating senior product judgment.

#### Scenario: Current reviewed set
- **WHEN** KudosCourts, CravingsPH, and Ample News are evaluated in their current reviewed states
- **THEN** KudosCourts leads, CravingsPH follows, and Ample News appears only with actions that pass proof-integrity validation

#### Scenario: Lead project loses readiness
- **WHEN** the current lead loses its reviewed artifact, public route, or factual publication readiness
- **THEN** it cannot remain first until the failed readiness condition is resolved

### Requirement: Hiring conversion is explicit and singular
The hero and Contact section SHALL state a factual senior/staff product-engineering interview invitation, and Contact SHALL make email the singular primary closing action while résumé and professional networks remain supporting utilities.

#### Scenario: Qualified visitor reaches Contact
- **WHEN** a recruiter or hiring manager reaches the closing section
- **THEN** they can determine the intended role conversation and activate the primary email action without interpreting a metaphor or choosing among equal-weight calls to action

#### Scenario: Supporting language uses metaphor
- **WHEN** wording such as “the difficult middle” remains on the page
- **THEN** literal role-fit and contact language appears first and the metaphor does not carry essential meaning by itself
