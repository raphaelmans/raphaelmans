## Required structure for future pages

1. Direct summary
2. Current status and personal role
3. Real-world problem and user workflow
4. Constraints
5. Personal ownership
6. Architecture and engineering decisions
7. Reliability and correctness
8. Verified outcome or observable product evidence
9. Honest limitations and next steps
10. Review date, internal links, and appropriate structured data

Thin placeholder routes, keyword-swapped pages, and bulk publication of the generated guide library are prohibited.
