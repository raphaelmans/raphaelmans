## Priority Issues

### [P2] Desktop prose exceeds the documented measure

**Why it matters:** Thirteen experience and decision paragraphs render at an estimated 88–97 characters per line, above the documented 65–75ch target. The information remains readable, but dense professional evidence becomes slower to scan.

**Fix:** Tighten the affected copy or reduce prose measure to approximately 65–72ch without weakening attribution, confidentiality boundaries, or decision specificity.

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable typeset`

### [P2] Observable results are quieter than architectural decisions

**Why it matters:** The decision evidence is excellent, but a screening visitor often needs to infer the operational consequence until opening a case study.

**Fix:** Add one short, disclosure-safe verification line per project—shipped state, reliability property, workflow enabled, or review boundary enforced—without inventing metrics.

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`

### [P3] The mobile proof journey remains long and mechanically repeated

**Why it matters:** The 390px homepage is about 7,146px tall. Fixed anchors make it navigable, but three similarly structured proof sequences delay Recognition and Contact.

**Fix:** Keep one flagship proof fully featured and vary the editorial compression of the other two, or introduce a quiet post-work Contact shortcut without duplicating the final close.

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable distill`
