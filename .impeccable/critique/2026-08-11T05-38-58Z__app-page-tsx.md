## Priority Issues

### [P1] Homepage evidence expands instead of progressively disclosing

**Why it matters:** The page measures roughly 5,216px on desktop and 7,511px at 390px. Five Ample News stages and four CravingsPH stages form narrow text towers on desktop and nine stacked cards on mobile, encouraging time-limited hiring managers to skim past the most consequential proof.

**Fix:** Keep ordered-list semantics but reduce homepage models to three decisive checkpoints or a compact left-rail sequence. Reserve full stage descriptions for case studies and remove inner card borders that do not communicate distinct state.

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable distill`, then `/impeccable adapt`.

### [P1] Verified employer credibility arrives below the first mobile viewport

**Why it matters:** The market position and specialty are clear, but the official current employer and 5+ years signal appear after the hero at 390px. A recruiter receives technical positioning before the fastest verifiable credibility cue.

**Fix:** Preserve the approved identity line, but tighten mobile hero spacing or add one compact current-role proof line in the supporting status copy so a credibility signal reaches the first viewport without competing with the primary CTA.

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`, then `/impeccable layout`.

### [P2] Desktop prose measures exceed the documented reading target

**Why it matters:** The rendered detector found 14 lines at roughly 88–97 characters, especially Experience summaries, proof points, employer-project summaries, owned decisions, and one evidence summary. This makes the already dense middle slower to scan.

**Fix:** Narrow long-form measures to approximately 65–75ch or tighten individual sentences. Do not shrink font size.

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable typeset`.

### [P2] Evidence landmark names are more verbose than their visible titles

**Why it matters:** Each evidence region uses a long sentence as `aria-label`, so assistive technology announces a verbose landmark instead of the concise visible heading.

**Fix:** Give each visible heading an ID, use `aria-labelledby` for the region name, and connect the explanation with `aria-describedby`.

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable audit`.

### [P3] The visual identity remains category-predictable

**Why it matters:** The restrained palette and typographic system are polished but familiar enough that visitors may remember the argument more than the site.

**Fix:** Introduce one evidence-derived boundary/connection motif and reduce repeated rounded rectangles. Preserve the established type and color identity rather than replacing it wholesale.

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable delight`.
